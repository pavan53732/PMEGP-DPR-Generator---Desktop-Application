# Non-Goals

To prevent scope creep and maintain the focus of **PMEGP DPR Pro**, the following are explicitly listed as non-goals for this software:

1. **Not Replacing CA Certification:** This software prepares the DPR for the bank. It does not replace the requirement for a Chartered Accountant's signature or certification on the final printed document if the bank mandates it.
2. **Not Filing PMEGP Applications:** The software generates the PDF application form. It does not integrate with the PMEGP e-Portal APIs to auto-submit applications on behalf of the user.
3. **Not Bank Underwriting Software:** The software calculates DSCR and ROI for the applicant. It does not assess credit risk, CIBIL scores, or act as an underwriting decision engine for the bank.
4. **Not a Legal Compliance Engine:** We do not automatically generate partnership deeds, MSME Udyam registrations, or GST filings.
5. **Not an Accounting ERP:** The financial projection engine (Layer 2) predicts the future 5 years. It is not designed to track daily ledgers, actual sales, inventory, or historical bookkeeping.
