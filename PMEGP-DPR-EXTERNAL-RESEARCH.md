## 5. DPR — Detailed Project Report Structural Template

The blueprint's 19-section structure (Project_Report rows 1-425) maps to the **10 standard sections** required by banks/KVIC:

| # | Standard DPR Section (per Sharda / Startup Guruz) | Maps to Blueprint § / Sheet Cells |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Executive Summary** (business idea, goals, financial projections summary) | `DPR_FRONT!B1` (`="Project Report on"` + UPPER applicant name) |
| 2 | **Promoter / Entrepreneur Profile** (name, address, qualifications, experience) | `Project_Report!B11`-`B14` (Constitution, Father's/Spouce's Name); `DataSheet!B8` (applicant.name); `DataSheet!B22` (qualification) |
| 3 | **Business Description** (manufacturing/service, location, products) | `Project_Report!B236`-`B307` section markers A-K (10 letter table); `DataSheet!B31` (project.name); `DataSheet!M80` (sector) |
| 4 | **Market Analysis** (target audience, demand/supply, competitor analysis) | `DataSheet!B94:G101` (8 product/sales lines for Year 1) |
| 5 | **Financial Plan** (project cost, means of finance, break-even, P&L, cash flow) | `DPR_print` rows 200-405 (full 5-year P&L, BEP, DSCR, cash flow) — the heart of the workbook |

### 5.1 DPR Common-Rejection Reasons (per Sharda Associates)

1. Project report **not in KVIC format** (i.e. not using the official `DPRPACKAGE.xls` template)
2. **Employment generation figures too low** or not justified in the report
3. **Machinery quotations missing** from unauthorized / unknown suppliers
4. **Unrealistic or inflated financial projections**
5. **Ineligible applicant** (existing business disguised as new)
6. **Duplicate applications** filed by the same person at multiple banks
7. **Subsidy percentage mismatch** (calculated in the report ≠ actual category)
8. Project cost **not aligned with PMEGP limits**

> Blueprint's `DPR-GUIDE-BLUEPRINT.md` §16.11 (cells the app must NEVER touch — locked values, B77 NOTE) directly addresses reasons 1 and 7.

## 6. Online Application Flow (KVIC e-Portal)

| Step | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register on **kviconline.gov.in/pmegp/pmegpweb** with mobile OTP | Sharda Associates; KVIC portal |
| 2 | Fill online Application Form A (personal + project details) | KVIC |
| 3 | Upload DPR + 5 mandatory documents (see §4 above) | KVIC eligibility_criteria page |
| 4 | Submit → routed to **KVIC / KVIB / DIC** (user-selected nodal agency) for verification | Sharda Associates |
| 5 | Nodal agency forwards to **bank branch** for credit appraisal | Sharda Associates |
| 6 | Bank sanctions loan → promoter deposits own contribution | Sharda Associates |
| 7 | Loan disbursed → subsidy credited to **TDR (Term Deposit Receipt) account** | Sharda Associates |
| Total time | **3 to 6 months** (varies by state, bank, DIC processing speed) | Sharda Associates |

## 8. KVIC Project-Profile Library (official, titles confirmed)

The official `kviconline.gov.in/pmegp/pmegpweb/docs/jsp/newprojectReports.jsp` page hosts a **KVIC-authored Project Profile** library (separate from the trade-catalog aggregators). Title was confirmed; full body not extractable in this scrape session.

The official `DPRPACKAGE.xls` template (the source workbook the blueprint is built from) is the **only** official KVIC DPR Excel template, and is described in the trade-catalog sites as:
- "Data Sheet is the main sheet for preparation & calculation for project report. You have to feed proper data wherever required i.e. in **Pista colour cell only**. Other fields are auto-calculated fields." — Vishwakarma Yojana
- "Pista colour cell" = **light-pistachio fill** (RGB ≈ 226, 239, 218), which is the **light cyan / light green fill** the Cline 2nd-pass xlrd-with-formatting_info scan found on G85/G86/G87 (RGB 204, 255, 255 light cyan) and surrounding M-column selectors. The blueprint's policy of "preserve these fills" is correct.

> **User-manual link** (referenced by Vishwakarma Yojana; body not extracted in this scrape): https://kviconline.gov.in/pmegp/pmegpweb/docs/dpr/PMEGP%20DPR%20User%20Manual_V1.1.pdf

## 10. Genuine new information worth adding to the blueprint

1. **TDR account for subsidy** — The subsidy is credited to a **Term Deposit Receipt (TDR) account** in the borrower's name at the bank, locked for 3 years (lock-in period). The blueprint §10.1 covers the lock-in period but doesn't explicitly name the TDR mechanism. **Recommend adding a 1-sentence note to §10.1.**
2. **1,050+ trade profiles covered** — Useful context for the app's "supported industries" UI. Currently in blueprint §6 / §15 implicitly (the workbook is trade-agnostic). **Recommend adding a one-liner to §3 or §6.**
3. **Pista-colour-cell UX convention** — The official KVIC template uses a **light-pistachio/light-cyan fill** (RGB ≈ 204, 255, 255 — confirmed by Cline 2nd-pass xlrd) on the data-entry cells. The blueprint's §15.3 documents the **G85/G86/G87** fills as light cyan (RGB 204, 255, 255) but doesn't generalize to "all data-entry cells share this fill". **Recommend adding to §15.3 / §17.3 a note that light-cyan fill is the data-entry convention across the workbook.**
4. **Reject reasons #5, #6, #7 (Sharda Associates)** — "Subsidy percentage mismatch" is a top-3 rejection reason. The blueprint §16.6 already documents the B77 mismatch as a UI warning; the new finding reinforces that this is a *known* high-frequency failure. **No new code action needed, but the §16.6 wording could be strengthened.**
